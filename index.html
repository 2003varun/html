Frequently asked questions

The questions we actually get before someone starts a trial project.

Is our data safe with you?

Data security and confidentiality is one of the commitments we operate under, and clients like SAP Ariba and Avery Dennison have vetted our data handling before signing on.

How do we know the work will be accurate before we commit?

That's what the free trial project is for — send a real batch, see the actual output, decide before anything ongoing starts.

What does this cost?

It depends on volume and which services you need — a one-time data cleanup prices differently from an ongoing monthly workflow. A short scoping call gets you a real number instead of a range that won't match your catalog anyway.

How fast can you turn work around?

Turnaround depends on the size and complexity of the batch — tell us your volume on a scoping call and we'll give you a real timeline, not a generic estimate.

Do we have to sign a long contract just to try this?

No. The free trial project is built to be low-risk — you're evaluating us as much as we're evaluating fit.

About Q-DAT

Q-DAT has spent sixteen years turning messy, inconsistent data into clean, structured records — product and catalogue data, customer and vendor records, transactions, documents and archives — for over a hundred clients in retail, manufacturing, healthcare, hospitality and financial services across India, the Gulf, Europe and North America. Alongside the data work, the team builds and supports the software and technology clients run it on. A Bangalore-based team handles the detail-heavy work — cataloguing, reconciling, verifying and digitizing — so client teams don't have to.
